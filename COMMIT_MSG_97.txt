#140 Fuel coach fired at 00:53 on a rest day with an empty food log
  "You're 153g of protein short ... after the long run ... ~40g in the next
  couple hours (and before bed)". Every clause was false at once: the session
  had not happened (primarySession is what the plan WROTE, not what was done),
  it was nearly 1am, and the "gap" from an empty log is just the target
  restated. gMechanism now has three gates - day.postWorkout (the MPS window
  must exist), day.fuelWindowOpen (the world model's 06:00-21:00 eating hours;
  an unknown clock leaves it CLOSED rather than guessing), and a protein.today
  presence check, the twin of gEnergyAvailability's. The beat is not deleted:
  a second wording says only what an empty log supports.

  Why it survived: coachQuality's TIME_SENSITIVE map had NO rule naming this
  beat, so timely=1.0 was vacuous for it; and coachNarrativeSim drew intake as
  rng.int(0,3200)/rng.int(0,180), making "exactly nothing logged" a ~0.5%
  coincidence - the no-data branch of every fuel beat was graded on ~zero
  cases. Both closed: a rule with teeth (proven by /tmp/proteinprobe.mjs
  asserting assessTimely FAILS at 01:00 and PASSES at 18:00) and an explicit
  15% emptyLog outcome with both numbers zero together.

#141/#142 both tier strips wrapped, for opposite reasons
  repeat(auto-fit, minmax(84px,1fr)) needs ~440px; a 390px phone wrapped it
  3+2 and clipped CEILING. Fixed in BOTH files the same way: an explicit
  repeat(N, minmax(0,1fr)) which can never wrap, clamp() typography so one
  declaration is 13.7px on a phone and 15.5px on web, and a maxWidth so the
  bins cannot balloon on a 1400px card. No media queries, no second layout.
  On web the biggest saving was deleting `asks 38` - it printed the selected
  option's peak a SECOND time ~90px from the chip already showing it, which
  is what pushed `Working` onto its own line.
